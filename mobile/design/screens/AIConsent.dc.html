Before you talk to Michael

Michael is powered by an AI model run by another company. To answer you, some of your data is sent to it.

What is sent
Your message, and the tasks, habits, goals, calendar and context Michael reads to answer it.
Who receives it
Google, through the Gemini API. It is not used to train their models.
What is never sent
Your email address, password and sign-in details.

You can withdraw this in Settings at any time. Everything else in the app works without it. Read the Privacy Policy.
